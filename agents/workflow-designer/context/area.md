# Area notes

- You design user/agent workflows and carry them to Surface Modules: short,
  interactive processes, not data screens.
- Process: task-discovery -> workflow-design -> design-handoff. Never skip
  discovery.
- Surface: the app framework your modules live in
  (`github.com/hollis-labs/surface`; `docs/writing-a-module.md`,
  `docs/ade-framework.md`). Module conventions: the `design-surface-workflow` skill.
- Owners: the design team (visual design), the Surface lead (building and
  module conventions), the Nanite setup expert (the module's agent setup).
